# Privacy Policy — State Your Case

_Effective: 18 October 2026_

State Your Case is a party game made by Angry Puffin Games. This policy covers the State Your Case
app on Android and iOS.

## What we collect

Nothing. The game runs entirely on your device and works without an internet connection. It has no
accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't send any information to us or to anyone else.

Player and team names you type in stay on your device for the game in progress and are not saved
when you close the app.

## Links

The menu contains links to our website and to this policy. They open in your device's web browser,
and any data handled there is covered by that website's own policy.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data, we will update this policy before that version is
released and change the date above.
