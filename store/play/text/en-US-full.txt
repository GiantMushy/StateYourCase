State Your Case is a party debate game for 3–8 players and a single phone.

One player becomes the Discussion Moderator. They pick the topic (Coffee or energy drinks? Is it ever okay to lie? Was the moon landing fake?) and decide what the speeches are judged on: comedy, creativity, staying on topic, facts or pure enthusiasm. Everyone else splits into teams or goes solo, gets a side, and makes their case against the clock.

But not everyone is playing fair. Before the debate the phone goes around the room, and each player secretly reveals their Corruption: a hidden objective worth bonus points. Say "and that's a fact" three times in your speech. Get the room clapping. Argue for the wrong side, then "correct" yourself. Ask the moderator how much time you have left – twice.

Think someone is up to something? Accuse them! Name their corruption and you steal their points. Guess wrong and it costs you.

When the talking stops, everyone votes and the scoreboard shows who argued best, and who got away with it. Three rounds, one winner.

• 3–8 players, one phone passed around the room
• Debate topics from silly to surprisingly deep
• Secret objectives that turn every speech into a bluff
• Accusations, votes and a scoreboard after every round
• Play in English or Icelandic
• No accounts, no ads, no internet connection needed
